# Source-supported thesis: financial foundation increment

The deployed eight-case post218 batch still returned four broad-thesis gaps.
This increment uses verified financial comparisons to explain a conditional
financial case, supporting signals, counter-evidence and monitoring conditions.
It is a partial foundation for a thesis; business description, competitive
position, valuation and issuer-disclosed operating-risk mechanisms remain open.

## Behavior

Broad questions such as “What is the investment thesis for Acadia Healthcare,
what supports it, and what could invalidate it? Cite material claims” stay in
the source-evidence route. Core financial retrieval is limited to revenue,
operating income, net income and operating cash flow rather than every metric.
At least two eligible comparisons are required to produce the foundation.

The composer rechecks the current and prior producer-bound facts against the
trusted ticker-to-CIK directory and regenerates their comparison with the
existing SEC metric producer. It requires exact agreement of claims, source
summary, source URL, publication date and reporting dates. Missing citations,
issuer mismatch, changed amounts, estimated values, future dates, conflicting
comparisons and stale/unavailable evidence are withheld. Filing metadata,
static profiles and generated summaries cannot form the financial case.

Each inference cites its reported inputs. A higher positive amount is treated
as a supporting financial signal; a decrease, unchanged amount or loss does
not establish improvement. A narrower loss is not described as positive
profitability. Conditional monitoring tests explicitly remain hypotheses, not
forecasts or issuer-disclosed risks. Differences in reporting durations or end
dates prevent promotion into a common-period margin, conversion ratio or
causal relationship.
Structured fact metadata also labels year-to-date duration observations as
year-to-date rather than treating every 10-Q observation as a quarter.

The response remains `partial` with explicit unanswered parts. It provides no
buy/sell recommendation, assessed conviction score or directional historical
thesis change. Existing account ownership, source admission, reference binding,
source-route model skipping and comparison gates stay in place. A segment,
issuer-KPI or named risk request continues through its scoped source gate;
company-wide financial comparisons cannot substitute for it.

## Validation and next acceptance

Tests cover favorable/mixed trends, loss reduction, quarter/year-to-date
differences, fact and citation tampering, trusted identity mismatch, conflicts,
segment/risk scope, and source-gate use for AAPL, AA, ACHC and TSLA. Pipeline
tests for AAPL and ACHC stub static profiles as absent and verify partial
financial answers without model calls or fabricated conviction.

After deployment, rerun the existing eight cases with a fresh report path.
Expect core-thesis cases with enough validated financial comparisons to become
partial financial foundations rather than complete thesis passes. Review all
facts and citations independently, preserve the financial-trend acceptance,
and inspect mixed signals and date ranges before recording usefulness results.

The next composition work needs issuer-bound business and risk disclosures,
business-specific mechanisms, valuation evidence, consistent research
personalization and unseen-company acceptance. The 100-issuer registry milestone
and full useful-answer quality gates remain unmet.
