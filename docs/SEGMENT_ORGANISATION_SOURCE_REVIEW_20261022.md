bounded segment discussion**, rather than promoting subsidiary activities into an
issuer-wide product fact. The AMTEC passage is an exposed development example,
not a ticker/alias/segment allowlist or independent quality pass.

1. Bind a complete eligible Business section and explicit segment opening/closing

   organisation; it is not a complete ownership chain.
