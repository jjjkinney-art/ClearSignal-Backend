## Boundaries and provenance

Ordinary 20-F reports require explicit Item 4 Information on the Company and
an explicit Item 4A Unresolved Staff Comments or Item 5 Operating and Financial
Review closing heading. Existing bounded scans reject TOC pagination, quoted
references, missing endings and oversized sections. Extraction and final binding
retain the actual 20-F form, source section, complete quote, offsets, publication
date and exact issuer URL. Existing 10-K evidence remains compatible.

For a foreign report whose business section follows risk factors, bounded text
selection preserves both complete sections and their closing headings if they
fit the existing 360,000-character budget. No download or router deadline is
increased. Unsupported annual amendments remain gaps.

ASML's integrated 2025 annual report uses its existing exact-URL/date/form/CIK
and canonical-hash registry. The source's 20-F cross-reference map identifies
business context in the strategic report. Added reviewed boundaries span the
lithography overview through products and services, ending before marketplace
commentary. Ingestion verifies the business mapping in full normalized source
text before any window selection. Business evidence carries the registry/hash
binding, which the final binder rechecks. No generic integrated-report override
or predecessor business inheritance is introduced.

A complete sentence beginning with a customer-purpose lead (To help our
customers ..., we provide/develop/...) may qualify if its main clause satisfies
the existing current-activity predicate. The full sentence remains quoted;
forecast, promotional, numeric, employee/internal and wrong-subject guards stay
in force. One explicit source heading can precede the customer-purpose sentence.
