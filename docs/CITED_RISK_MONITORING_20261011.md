## Change

After independent disclosure binding and response-reference admission, the financial thesis composer can add a risk_monitoring_inference for an explicit condition/effect sentence. The narrow grammar recognizes could/may adversely affect, could/may have a material adverse effect on, and could/may harm. It preserves the actual condition and effect fragments and cites the same admitted disclosure. Missing effects and nested modal conditions produce no inference rather than a guessed mechanism.

The conditional test asks whether the disclosed condition occurs and whether the issuer reports the specified adverse operating effects. Confirmed deterioration would challenge an assumption that the risk remains manageable. The disclosure itself does not establish occurrence, financial causation, risk probability or complete thesis invalidation. Historical predecessor conditions require current successor relevance confirmation first and remain explicitly historical.

The structured answer distinguishes financial_inference from risk_monitoring_inference. No new reported fact, risk ranking, probability, numerical threshold or valuation conclusion is generated. The existing risk materiality/likelihood/effect gap stays open, and the source-answer status remains partial. This increment adds conditional research utility; it is not a completion of the risk-analysis workstream or proof of launch readiness.
