TSMC (TSM) — Geopolitical Transmission Channel

TSMC trades at a discount to peers partly on geopolitical risk. Answer the following:

1. What is the primary channel by which a Taiwan Strait military escalation would transmit to TSMC's revenue and earnings — operational disruption, customer demand destruction, or capital market re-rating?

2. What discount to intrinsic value is the market currently applying to TSMC's multiple specifically due to geopolitical risk, and how was that estimate derived?

3. What specific announcement or data point would most credibly reduce that discount?
