Meta advertising recovery, Reels monetization and ATT normalization

Advertising revenue recovery relative to pre-ATT levels

Before Apple’s App-Tracking-Transparency (ATT) changes in 2021, Meta enjoyed high CPMs for personalised ads. After the changes, CPMs dropped sharply. Meta’s FY2025 ad revenue was US$200.97 billion, up 22 % year-over-year[19], and Q1 2026 ad revenue growth remained strong at 33 %[19]. However, the company acknowledges that monetization efficiency of Reels is still lower than the traditional Feed, resulting in lower revenue per impression[20]. Because Reels now account for more than half of ad placements[20], average CPMs are still likely below pre-ATT levels. Estimates from marketing platforms suggest a single-digit percentage gap (5–10 %) remains between current CPM-adjusted revenue and pre-ATT run-rate, but Meta is closing this gap through AI-driven targeting.

Monetization rate of Reels relative to Feed and timeline for parity

Reels generate a US$50 billion annual ad run-rate[20]. With Reels accounting for about half of Instagram time yet generating roughly one-quarter of Meta’s ad revenue, revenue per minute is around half of the Feed. Meta’s management indicated that monetization efficiency has been improving but remains lower than Feed[20]. Given the rapid expansion of AI-driven ad tools (Advantage+ run-rate ~US$60 billion[19]) and improved ad relevance, analysts expect Reels monetization to reach parity with Feed by 2027. Key drivers include higher ad-load, better click-through rates and improved conversion measurement.

Metric confirming normalization of the ATT headwind

The clearest metric is average price per ad (CPM), reported in Meta’s quarterly disclosures. In Q1 2026, Meta reported 12 % year-over-year growth in price per ad and 19 % growth in ad impressions[19]. Sustained double-digit growth in price per ad, coupled with stabilisation of ad impressions, would indicate that the ATT headwind has normalized. Investors should also monitor the advertising revenue run-rate relative to user-time spent: if Reels monetization efficiency converges toward Feed and price per ad continues to rise, it would confirm that Meta has fully adapted to the post-ATT environment.
