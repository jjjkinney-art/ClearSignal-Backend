Here's the full analytical framework on all three questions.
The dollar range at realistic risk — and why it's tiered
Google Search and other generated roughly $224 billion across full-year 2025, and Q1 2026 Search revenue hit $60.4 billion, up 19% year-over-year — an acceleration. The substitution risk is not uniform across this base. PPC LandSearch Engine Journal
The critical structural insight is that Google retains dominance on navigational and transactional queries — AI-native tools now capture a meaningful share of research and informational queries, but the distribution is almost inverted: informational and research queries are 58% of ChatGPT Search volume, but only 3% are purely transactional. Since transactional and commercial queries drive the overwhelming majority of Google's CPC-based revenue, the substitution risk is concentrated in the lowest-monetized part of the query mix. Digital Applied Team
For the 15-20% of search revenue tied to pure informational queries, transactional queries still only trigger AI Overviews about 5-14% of the time while informational queries trigger them 36-50% of the time — but informational queries were already the lowest-CPC tier. Realistic revenue at risk over 24 months: $10-25 billion, or roughly 4-11% of current search revenue. The bear end of that range requires AI-native tools to meaningfully penetrate commercial investigation queries in a way that isn't happening yet. SeoProfy
The primary mechanism of loss — the counterintuitive answer
It is not fewer queries. Total information discovery sessions grew 26% worldwide from Q1 2023 to Q4 2025 — AI tools are expanding the information-seeking market, not displacing Google's absolute volume. Dojoai
It is not lower CPCs in the near term either. The paradox is that AI Overviews are actually driving CPC inflation: organic CTR dropped nearly 34.5% when AI Overviews appear, pushing advertisers into paid search and driving average CPC increases of 28% across most commercial categories. Mediaplus clients saw CPCs rise 10-15% within the last year. AdExpertDigiday
The primary mechanism over 24 months is advertiser budget reallocation away from mid-funnel commercial intent. OpenAI is targeting $2.5 billion in advertising revenue for 2026, and if AI-native platforms develop credible ad products that intercept the research and consideration phase of the purchase journey, performance marketers will shift budgets at the margin — not because Google's queries fell, but because advertisers believe AI tools are reaching intent earlier and more cost-effectively. PPC Land
The defense metric and what level reassures the market
The most precise metric is implied Search revenue per query. Google doesn't disclose query volume directly, but Alphabet's CFO confirmed on the Q1 2026 call that &quot;the queries continue to grow and are at an all-time high,&quot; and that &quot;AI Overviews and AI Mode continue to drive greater Search usage and growth in overall queries, including in commercial queries.&quot; The combination of this disclosure with Search revenue growth gives an implied revenue-per-query trajectory. AI Overviews are monetizing at a rate similar to traditional Search, per management commentary, and Pichai noted AI Mode queries run 3× longer than traditional searches — creating more ad surface area per session. Yahoo Finance + 2
The reassurance level: Search revenue growing at 15%+ YoY for two or three consecutive quarters while Google's own AI products (AI Mode, AI Overviews) are simultaneously disclosed as scaling in query volume. That combination means Google is extracting more commercial value per search interaction — not less — as the AI era deepens. That directly falsifies the substitution thesis. Search revenue growth accelerated every single quarter in 2025 at exactly the same time AI Overviews and AI Mode were scaling globally and ChatGPT was hitting 810 million monthly users — which is what the current data shows. The bear case requires that trajectory to reverse, and there is no evidence of that happening in the reported numbers through Q1 2026. SaaStr
