The leading indicator with the longest lead time
Airline forward booking data leads Visa's cross-border ex-EU volume by the greatest margin — approximately 1-2 quarters. The mechanism is direct: international flights are booked 8-12 weeks before travel, the card is charged at booking, and Visa's cross-border volume is recognized when the transaction crosses a currency border. IATA traffic data and OAG seat capacity schedules provide approximately 2-3 months of forward visibility into the exact corridors that drive Visa's highest-yielding cross-border transactions.
