Q01 (NVIDIA):
If the top four hyperscalers reduce aggregate capex growth by
20 percentage points year-over-year, what is the estimated
dollar-range impact on NVIDIA's Data Center segment revenue?
What is the single most important operating metric to monitor
that would give advance warning of this deceleration? At what
level of that metric does the bull thesis on NVDA break?

Q02 (Costco):

expansion, and why is it not a typical retail merchandise-mix
story? What metric best tracks the durability of this expansion
on a quarterly basis? What would cause the expansion to reverse,
and what is the threshold at which investors should reconsider
the premium valuation?

Q03 (JPMorgan Chase):
What is the dollar threshold of CRE loan losses that would
become material to JPM's earnings — either requiring additional
reserve builds or affecting the dividend/buyback program? What
is JPM's current CRE office provision coverage ratio, and how
does it compare to pre-GFC norms? What CRE delinquency rate —
expressed as a percentage — would trigger a consensus estimate
revision for JPM's full-year EPS?

Q04 (TSMC):
What is the primary channel by which a Taiwan Strait military
escalation would transmit to TSMC's revenue and earnings —
operational disruption, customer demand destruction, or capital
market re-rating? What discount to intrinsic value is the
market currently applying to TSMC's multiple specifically due
to geopolitical risk, and how was that estimate derived? What
specific announcement or data point would most credibly reduce
that discount?

Q05 (Meta Platforms):
Has Meta fully recovered its advertising revenue run rate to
pre-ATT levels on a CPM-adjusted basis, and if not, what is
the remaining gap in percentage terms? What is the current
monetization rate of Reels relative to Feed (expressed as
revenue per minute of watch time or equivalent metric), and
what is the timeline for parity? What single metric in Meta's
quarterly earnings would most clearly confirm that the ATT
headwind has permanently normalized?

Q06 (Microsoft):
What annual revenue run rate does Azure Copilot need to reach
to be material to Microsoft's fiscal year EPS — define
"material" as contributing at least 1 percentage point to EPS
growth? What enterprise seat attachment rate is implied by that
revenue target, and what is the current observed attachment
rate? If enterprise AI adoption stalls at current attachment
rates, what is the downside to consensus FY2026 Azure growth
estimates, expressed in percentage points?

Q07 (Amazon):
Is Amazon AWS operating margin expansion structural (driven by
pricing power and mix shift to higher-margin services) or
cyclical (driven by infrastructure cost optimization post-
overinvestment)? What is the current AWS operating margin, and
at what level does continued expansion require either new
pricing power or a step-change in AI infrastructure efficiency?
What quarterly metric — operating income dollars, margin
percentage, or revenue growth rate — would most clearly signal
that the expansion has peaked?

Q08 (Alphabet):

queries, lower CPCs, or advertiser budget reallocation? What
